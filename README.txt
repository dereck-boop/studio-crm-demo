Studio CRM demo (static)
========================

A click-through demo of the Quiethouse studio CRM with made-up bands, clients,
bookings and invoices. Nothing is saved and no real customer data is included.

Start page: index.html (the dashboard)
Also worth showing: book.html (client booking page) and band.html (private band page)

To put it online, upload this whole folder (all .html files plus the assets folder)
to any web host. No server, database or build step is needed. You can also just
double-click index.html to open it on your own computer.

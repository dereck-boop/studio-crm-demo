Everything else

These parts of the CRM aren't in the demo, but here's what each one does.

Money and admin

Tax Time

Year-end totals, 1099 list for anyone you paid, and income from royalties in one place.

Business Deadlines

Quarterly taxes, insurance renewals and licenses, with email reminders.

Expenses

Log costs with a receipt photo; profit shows on the dashboard and reports.

Packages & Gift Cards

Sell a 5-session block or a gift card; balances come off invoices automatically.

Royalties & Income

Track points, royalty statements and other income alongside studio work.

People

Producers / Engineers

See every band a producer has sent you, with their own private page.

Companies

Labels, managers and venues, linked to their artists.

Network

Session players, mastering engineers and photographers you refer people to.

DM Inbox

Instagram messages turn into leads in one click.

Notifications

New requests, payments, invoice views and band messages.

Studio

Events

Release shows, workshops and closed days, on the calendar and a private calendar feed.

To-Dos

Per project or general, with a daily email digest.

Time

Start/stop timers on a project or artist; hours roll into invoices.

Files & Review

Mixes and client notes live on the studio's review site, linked from each project.

Equipment

Gear list with values for insurance, reservations on bookings, barcode check-out and a damage log.

Studios

Your rooms and partner studios, with which ones clients can request online.

Growth

Content

Plan posts and get AI drafts from finished projects.

Links & Bio

A link-in-bio page with tracked links that show which posts bring clients.

Social Stats

Instagram and YouTube numbers over time.

Newsletter

Email your list about openings and new releases.

Portfolio & Reviews

A public page of your credits and client testimonials.

Releases

Release checklists and pre-save links for client records.

Sync pitches

Track songs pitched to film, TV and ads.

Goals

Yearly targets shown on the dashboard.

Setup

Price List

Services and rates that fill quotes and invoices.

Web Forms

Build forms and embed them on your website.

Emails

Edit every automatic email: requests, reminders, receipts, thank-yous.

Agreement

A recording agreement clients accept online before their session.

Studio Settings

Name, logo, payment options, deposit rules, policies, two-step login and Google Calendar sync.
